How To Download

Three quick steps and you're watching.

1

Find Your Title

Search or browse to the anime, movie or series you want, open its page, and scroll down to Download Links.

2

Pick a Quality

Tap 480p / 720p / 1080p. Any quality marked "Not Available" isn't ready for that title yet.

3

Download Starts

You'll briefly land on a redirect page, then your download begins automatically. Close any extra ad/tab that pops up and come back here.

⚠

Running into a problem?

If a link isn't working, let us know on the Report Link Not Working page.
